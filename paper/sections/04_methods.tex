\section{Methods and Experimental Design}
The final protocol will define the prediction target, population, estimand,
primary metric, and evaluation scheme before model selection. Candidate
classification algorithms are logistic regression, nearest neighbors,
decision trees, random forests, and support vector machines, alongside a
dummy baseline. Regression would require appropriate alternative estimators.
\subsection{Preprocessing and Validation}
Imputation, scaling, encoding, feature selection, and any resampling will
be fitted only on the training portion of each split. All algorithms will
share the same evaluation splits. Grouped or temporal data will require
corresponding validation constraints. The final test data will be reserved
until hyperparameters, calibration, and decision thresholds are fixed.
\subsection{Interpretation and Reproducibility}
The selected model will be interpreted with a suitable method and compared
with an interpretable baseline. Predictive associations will not be treated
as causal effects. Seeds, environment versions, data hashes, configurations,
and execution instructions will accompany the code.
