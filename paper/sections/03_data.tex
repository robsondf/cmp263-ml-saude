\section{Data and Study Population}
Two Brazilian sources are initial candidates: the SIVEP-Gripe severe acute
respiratory syndrome records~\cite{msSivep} and the 2019 National Health
Survey microdata~\cite{ibgePns}. Neither source has been selected or downloaded.
The final paper will retain references to the selected source and report
the download URL, snapshot date, license or terms, dictionary, eligibility
criteria, sample size, target distribution, exclusions, and missing values.
The study unit and the availability of predictors at the stated prediction
time must be documented before training.
